# SpaceBody: The Astronaut Health Explorer

## Project Overview

**SpaceBody: The Astronaut Health Explorer** is an interactive
educational platform designed to explain how long-duration space
missions affect the human body and how NASA research helps protect
astronauts during future exploration missions.

The project transforms complex space medicine research into an
easy-to-understand visual experience where users can explore different
human body systems and learn about the challenges astronauts face in
microgravity environments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Statement

Human beings evolved to live on Earth under normal gravity, atmosphere,
and environmental conditions. When astronauts travel into space for
extended periods, their bodies experience major physical and
psychological changes.

Future missions to the Moon, Mars, and deep space require solutions for:

-   Bone density loss
-   Muscle weakening
-   Vision problems
-   Mental health challenges
-   Radiation exposure effects

Understanding these challenges is essential for safe human space
exploration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Project Goal

The goal of SpaceBody is to create an interactive learning experience
that helps people understand:

-   How space changes the human body
-   Why astronauts face health risks
-   How NASA researches these problems
-   What solutions are being developed for future missions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Concept

SpaceBody provides a virtual astronaut health explorer.

Users can interact with a human body model and select different body
systems to learn:

1.  The space-related problem
2.  The effect on astronauts
3.  NASA research findings
4.  Possible solutions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Main Modules

## 1. Bone Health Explorer

### Challenge:

Microgravity reduces the mechanical load on bones.

### Effect:

-   Bone density decreases
-   Higher fracture risk after returning to Earth

### NASA Solutions:

-   Exercise programs
-   Nutrition strategies
-   Medical monitoring

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Muscle Health Explorer

### Challenge:

Astronauts do not use muscles the same way as they do on Earth.

### Effect:

-   Muscle loss
-   Reduced physical strength

### NASA Solutions:

-   Resistance training equipment
-   Regular astronaut exercise routines

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Vision Health Explorer

### Challenge:

Fluid shifts in microgravity can affect eye structure.

### Effect:

-   Vision changes
-   Spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular issues

### NASA Solutions:

-   Eye monitoring
-   Human spaceflight medical research

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Mental Health Explorer

### Challenge:

Long missions create isolation, stress, and sleep disruption.

### Effect:

-   Psychological stress
-   Mood changes
-   Reduced performance

### NASA Solutions:

-   Crew support systems
-   Behavioral health research
